## About Me

I am a **PhD researcher in Computational Mathematics** at the **School of Mathematics and Statistics, Central South University (CSU), Changsha, China**.

My research focuses on the development, mathematical analysis, and implementation of efficient iterative algorithms for solving large-scale nonlinear equations and optimization problems.

I am particularly interested in designing accelerated numerical methods with strong theoretical convergence properties and practical computational efficiency. My research combines mathematical analysis with computational experiments and real-world applications in image restoration, signal processing, machine learning, and motion control.

## Research Interests

- **Numerical Optimization:** Large-scale optimization, nonlinear optimization, and constrained optimization.
- **Monotone Operator Theory:** Monotone nonlinear equations, convex constraints, and operator-splitting algorithms.
- **Derivative-Free Methods:** Projection methods, conjugate-gradient methods, spectral methods, and quasi-Newton techniques.
- **Accelerated Algorithms:** Inertial methods, hybrid algorithms, double-step-length strategies, and restart techniques.
- **Applications:** Image restoration, sparse signal reconstruction, machine learning, and motion control.
